
## SCOPE & PRE-FILTER
- Reply scan: in:inbox newer_than:30d, minus automated senders (notifications@*, noreply@*,
  reminder@superhuman.com, *@docusign.net, drive-shares-dm-noreply@google.com,
  notifications@calendly.com, newsletters). The "commitments I made" scan (unsent docs /
  unkept promises) uses the same 30d window.
- READ STATE IS MEANINGLESS: Shimon archives what he has processed - a thread still in INBOX
  is unhandled regardless of read/unread. Never use is:unread or is:read as a signal.
- SENT-ONLY / SELF-INITIATED scan (CLOSES A COVERAGE GAP - a thread I STARTED that has no
  reply yet lives in Sent, NOT Inbox, so the in:inbox reply-scan never sees it; e.g. the
  "Copperhelm + Security Hub Extended - next step" thread to Beville/Amazon was missed exactly
  this way): ALSO scan in:sent newer_than:7d. (The 7-30 day sent window is owned by the separate deep-sent-scan routine that runs Mon + Thu - do not scan it here; the two routines coordinate via shared drafts and the READY-TO-ARCHIVE label.) For every sent thread where (a) my message is
  the latest/only message with NO reply back, (b) no meeting is booked with that person, (c) no
  draft and no READY-TO-ARCHIVE tag already exist on it, and (d) the message EXPECTS a response
  - a next-step, a question, a scheduling ask, or outreach awaiting reply (NOT a pure FYI,
  forward, closure, receipt, or internal @copperhelm.com note) - feed it into Gate 1b exactly
  like an inbox thread: if 3+ BUSINESS DAYS silent, DRAFT a nudge (with the mandatory slot grid
  if it is a scheduling thread). De-dupe by threadId against the inbox scan so each thread is
  handled once. Subject to all the same gates (PRE-DRAFT CALENDAR + RECENCY, teammate, never-flag).
  PAGINATION IS MANDATORY: the API pages at 50 and a single bulk campaign can fill a page on its
  own, so even a 7-day sent window can span multiple pages. You MUST page through EVERY page
  (follow nextPageToken until exhausted). Note a
  single bulk campaign can bury a week of real threads on its own (~30 Black Hat emails sent within
  two minutes did precisely that). RECONCILE: report the total sent-thread count AND the number
  actually evaluated; if they differ, fetch the missing pages before reporting. Real threads missed
  this way: "Temi Abedambo - Copperhelm Advisory" (Jul 24, advisor ask), "MS Xbox - Copperhelm"
  (Jul 20, Megan O'Neil intro), "Lululemon - Copperhelm follow up" (Jul 9).
- IGNORE reminder@superhuman.com entirely (deprecated; pollutes results) - never count it
  as activity or as the latest message.
- THREAD_VIEW_MINIMAL truncates long threads to their OLDEST ~5 messages - re-verify the
  true latest message (METADATA_ONLY / FULL_CONTENT) for any thread at that ceiling. NEVER
  take a replyToMessageId from this view (see DRAFTS > THREADING).
- BUSINESS DAYS definition (used everywhere below - REVISED 2026-08-06, one day faster): business
  days are Mon-Fri. A thread becomes nudge-eligible ON THE 3RD BUSINESS DAY after my last
  message (the send day itself never counts). Examples: sent Monday -> eligible on the THURSDAY
  morning run; sent Sunday -> Mon and Tue elapse -> eligible on the WEDNESDAY morning run.
  Weekend sends never pay an extra-day penalty (real case: a Sunday Aug 2 NDA nudge to
  Cameron/PwC was not eligible until Thursday under the old "3 FULL weekdays elapsed" wording).
